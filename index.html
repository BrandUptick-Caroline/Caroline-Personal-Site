THE PERSON BEHIND BRAND UPTICK

Off screen, I'm still building.

The portfolio shows what I make. This page is about who I am while I'm making it — the school years that shaped my discipline, the leadership roles I've held, and the painting, photography, and music I do simply because I love them.

01 — Who I Am

Discipline, on and off the timetable.

I was on stage before I could really remember it — an All-Island Winner at the Sri Lanka Festival of Music, Dance & Speech at age 3. Performing, leading, and competing have been part of my life ever since.

Long before IT and design, school gave me my first real lessons in leadership and consistency — from marching in a junior band to eventually being trusted to lead as Senior Head Prefect. I spent my school years moving between uniformed groups, the stage, and the classroom — leading a band, wearing the Girl Guide badge for years, performing in drama and recitation competitions, and finally stepping into prefectship. Each of those rooms taught me something different: the band taught timing, Guides taught service, drama taught presence, and prefectship taught responsibility. That same mix — structure, creativity, and follow-through — is exactly what carries into how I design and build today as the founder of Brand UpTick.
